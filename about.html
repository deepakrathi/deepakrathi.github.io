About Android Trail

Android Trail is an independent app studio focused on building small, useful Android apps. We keep things simple: one clear purpose per app, minimal permissions, and no unnecessary clutter.

Our current apps span casual gaming and everyday utilities — from Ludo Legends, a multiplayer take on the classic board game, to Mala Counter, a distraction-free mantra counter for meditation practice. More apps are on the way.

What we believe

Apps should respect the people who use them. That means being upfront about what data we collect (as little as possible), being honest in our app store listings, and building things we'd want to use ourselves.
