Delete your data

If you'd like us to delete the data associated with your account or usage of one of our apps, submit a request below. We'll process it and delete your data within 72 hours.

Use the same email address associated with the app, if any. We only use this to confirm and process your deletion request.

What gets deleted

We'll remove any account information, usage data, or identifiers tied to your submitted email address for the selected app, in line with that app's privacy policy. Some apps (like Mala Counter) don't collect any data in the first place, so there may be nothing to delete — we'll let you know either way if you've asked us to confirm.
